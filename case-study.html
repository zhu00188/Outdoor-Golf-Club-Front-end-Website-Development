Overview

Creating a Digital Foundation for a Premium Golf Startup

Recognizing that most golf clubs struggle with outdated, non-responsive websites that fail to attract modern members, I designed and developed a comprehensive digital solution for Golf Space, a premium golf club startup. The goal was to create a responsive web application that delivers a seamless, intuitive user experience across all devices and browsers.

Project Purpose

To establish a strong digital presence for a premium golf club startup that positions them as a modern, accessible, and professional organization, enabling them to compete with established clubs and attract a diverse membership base.

Core Challenges

Most golf club websites suffer from poor mobile optimization, confusing navigation, lack of online booking systems, and inaccessible designs. As a startup, Golf Space had no existing website, brand identity, or design system—requiring everything to be built from scratch.

Delivered Results

10+ fully responsive web pages, comprehensive atomic design system, 40+ reusable components, WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance, 97% Lighthouse accessibility score, and complete technical documentation.

Problem & Solution

Challenges, Solutions & Deliverables

Key Challenges

  • Building from Scratch: No existing website, brand guidelines, or design system—required creating complete structure and visual identity from ground zero.
  • Industry-Wide UX Problems: Mainstream golf websites suffer from poor mobile optimization, confusing navigation, and outdated aesthetics that fail to attract younger demographics.
  • Accessibility Gaps: Most golf club websites ignore WCAG/AODA standards, excluding users with disabilities and limiting potential membership reach.
  • Multiple User Groups: Needed to serve diverse audiences (corporate executives, young professionals, retirees) with vastly different needs and technical proficiency.
  • Scalability Requirements: System must accommodate future growth in services, content, and features without requiring complete redesign.

My Solutions

  • Atomic Design System: Built a comprehensive design system from tokens to templates for scalability and consistency.
  • User Journey Mapping: Created distinct user flows for each audience segment with clear CTAs.
  • Component-Based Architecture: Developed 40+ reusable components with variants for flexibility.
  • Accessibility-First Approach: Integrated WCAG AA compliance into every design decision and code implementation.
  • Responsive Grid System: Implemented Bootstrap 5 with custom breakpoints optimized for all devices.

My Design & Development Process

I applied a systematic approach combining user research, iterative design, and technical implementation to deliver the final website. The process included:

1 - Research & Discovery

To design a premium digital experience for Golf Space, I began by understanding both the market and the users. I conducted competitive benchmarking across 15+ high-end golf club websites to identify industry patterns, common user expectations, and functional standards. At the same time, desk and online research helped uncover emerging trends and digital behaviors, while client-facing discovery clarified business goals and operational priorities. These insights were synthesized into three distinct personas, each representing a primary audience segment.

WCAG & AODA Compliant

Color Contrast

All text meets minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text and 3:1 for large text.

  • Primary (#344e41) on white: 10.2:1 (AAA)
  • Body text (#212121) on white: 15.8:1 (AAA)
  • Accent (#f4732a) on white: 4.8:1 (AA)
  • Secondary (#a3b18a) on dark: 5.3:1 (AA)

Screen Reader Support

Comprehensive ARIA labels and semantic HTML for assistive technology compatibility.

  • Semantic HTML5 elements (nav, main, footer)
  • ARIA landmarks and live regions
  • Alt text for all decorative and functional images
  • Descriptive link and button labels

Responsive & Mobile

Touch-friendly targets (minimum 44x44px) and optimized mobile experience.

  • All interactive elements meet 44x44px minimum
  • Adequate spacing between clickable elements
  • Responsive text sizing (16px minimum)
  • Tested across iOS and Android devices

Content Readability

Clear typography hierarchy and readable font sizes optimized for all users.

  • Base font size: 16px (body text)
  • Line height: 1.6-1.8 for readability
  • Clear visual hierarchy with heading levels
  • Proper paragraph spacing and margins
